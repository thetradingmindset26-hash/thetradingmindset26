20 Emotional Mistakes Every Trader Makes
Read each one. Ask yourself: how many happened this week?
20
MISTAKES
01
FOMO Entry
Entering because you saw it move — not because you planned it.
FIX
If it was not on your watchlist before market open — you cannot enter. No exceptions.
02
Revenge Trading
Taking a trade to recover money you just lost.
FIX
2 consecutive losses = trading day ends. Close the platform immediately. No re-entry.
03
Moving Stop Loss
Widening your stop because you think it will come back.
FIX
Your stop is a contract. Set it at entry. Never move it. The trader who set it was calmer.
04
Early Exit
Exiting a winning trade before your target out of fear.
FIX
Set target as a hard order before entry. Do not look at the trade until stop or target hits.
05
Late Exit
Holding a losing trade past your stop hoping it reverses.
FIX
When your stop hits — exit. No thinking. No hoping. That is what the stop is for.
06
Overtrading
Taking 8 trades on a day your strategy gives 2 setups.
FIX
Max 3 trades per day. Every trade beyond that is boredom masquerading as opportunity.
07
Oversizing
Using a position size that makes you emotional about the outcome.
FIX
Risk only what you can lose without it affecting your next decision. Use the Risk Calculator.
08
Trading When Tired
Trading after less than 6 hours of sleep.
FIX
Below 6 hours sleep = no live trading. Paper trade or do not trade. This is neuroscience.
09
Post-Win Overconfidence
Overconfidence after a large win leads to sloppy entries.
FIX
After a big win — reduce position size 50% for the next session. Protect the edge.
10
Ignoring Higher Timeframe
Entering on 5-min while 15-min and 1-hour are against you.
FIX
Check HTF structure before every entry. If it conflicts — do not trade.
11
Breaking Your Rules
Taking a trade you know violates your system.
FIX
Write your rules. Before entry — does this pass every rule? If no — skip it.
12
No Plan
Entering without a defined entry, stop, target and reason.
FIX
No plan = no trade. Takes 2 minutes. Write it before you click. Use the Risk Calculator.
13
Watching P&L Mid-Trade
Checking unrealised P&L while in a trade.
FIX
Set stop and target as hard orders. Close the P&L column. Walk away. Let the trade work.
14
Trading News Without Prep
Entering into high-impact news without understanding the risk.
FIX
Check economic calendar every morning. Avoid trading 15 min before/after major news events.
15
Comparing P&L to Others
Seeing profit screenshots and increasing size to match.
FIX
Your benchmark is your own historical average. Comparison is a losing game. Ignore screenshots.
16
Not Reviewing Trades
Taking trades without logging or reviewing them.
FIX
Log every trade. Run Coach Report after every 5 trades. Read it. Act on one thing.
17
Anchoring to Entry Price
Holding a losing trade because you need to get back to breakeven.
FIX
The market does not know where you entered. Would I enter RIGHT NOW? If no — exit.
18
Trading to Prove Something
Trading to prove to yourself or others that you can make money.
FIX
Your only job each session is to follow your rules. Money is a byproduct of discipline.
19
Ignoring Daily Loss Limit
Continuing after hitting your pre-set daily loss limit.
FIX
When daily limit hits — platform closes. No exceptions. Honour the rule you made when calm.
20
Not Trusting Your System
Skipping valid setups or reducing size after recent losses.
FIX
Trust your system for 20 consecutive trades before judging it. Inconsistency is the leak.
The data shows which of these you actually do.
